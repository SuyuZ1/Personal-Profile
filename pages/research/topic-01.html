RESEARCH QUESTION / 001

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

Can a data system understand its own world, identify what limits its growth, and autonomously organize what should evolve next?

High-quality data is increasingly expensive to acquire. Human-designed pipelines are approaching their scaling limits, while multi-source data remains fragmented and difficult to continuously enrich. This research asks whether data infrastructure can move beyond passive execution and learn to direct its own evolution through continuous feedback.

RESEARCH VISION / 003

DATA INFRASTRUCTURE

A unified substrate for organizing the evolution of an entire data world.

The system no longer only stores and processes existing data. It progressively learns to understand the state of its data world, determine how that world should grow, and continuously adjust the path of evolution through feedback.

Individual capabilities are developed through inheritance and closed-loop coordination, gradually transforming passive infrastructure into an intelligent data world that can expand and increase its own value.
